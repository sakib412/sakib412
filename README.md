I spend most of my time on **distributed systems and cloud architecture** — multi-tenant design, event-driven pipelines (transactional outbox, SQS, EventBridge, idempotent consumers), payment infrastructure, and the AWS infrastructure (managed in Pulumi) that keeps it all running.

## 🔭 What I'm up to

- Scaling **[EzyCourse](https://ezycourse.com)** & **[EzyCommunity](https://ezycommunity.com/)**, a multi-tenant LMS + community platform used by thousands of creator businesses
- Founding architect of **EzyCourse 2.0**, a from-scratch rewrite in Go on PostgreSQL: transactional outbox, job system, two-level in-memory + Redis cache, and code-defined RBAC. Load-tested before launch at ~27k writes/s (4M answers from a simulated 100k-learner exam, zero errors)
- Owning our AWS infrastructure as code (Pulumi + TypeScript) across 13 products: ECS on Fargate/Fargate Spot, Lambda, SQS, EventBridge, RDS, with Grafana + Loki for observability
- Leading and growing the engineering team at **AppifyLab**
